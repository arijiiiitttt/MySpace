Walk around a pixel-art space with friends or teammates. Step close to someone to talk with voice and video, hop on a computer to share your screen, or just sit down and chat.

Enter My Space How it works

No sign-up. Pick a character, pick a name, walk in.

Walk up & talk

Voice and video connect automatically when you get close and fade as you walk away, like a real office.

Share your screen

Sit at a computer and present to everyone who is at the same desk. No meeting links.

Private rooms

Make up a room name, add a password, and send your team the invite link.

Works on your phone

Touch joystick, tap-to-walk and a compact layout. Nothing to install.
